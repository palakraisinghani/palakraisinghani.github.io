RL, Self-Play, Coevolution, Opponent Diversity
This project in RL explores several RL algorithms and training approaches - fixed opponent, self play, co-evolution etc. Turns out training against the same fixed opponent forever has a ceiling - the agent gets really good at beating that one opponent and not much else. Self play and co-evolution close that gap. Finally a well trained AlphaZero model is packed behind a casino theme UI and you can see it here.
Over Parameterized Models
Spring 2026
Gradient descent, Implicit bias, Overfitting, Generalization
Why dont overparameterized models overfit data and perform poorly on test data? We look at implicit bias of gradient descent at play in overparameterized models, analyzing their learning behavior via the Label-Randomization experiment and verify their double descent behavior.
