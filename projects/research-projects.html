Decentralized Information Flow Control in Distributed Systems
Autumn 2025 - Present
Computer Systems Security Laboratory, IISc
System Security, Cryptography, DIFC
The lack of a central authority in communication systems can create a serious gap in decentralized information control. Cryptographic primitives help narrow this gap by providing certain guarantees even in mutually distrustful settings. We extend previous work on confidentiality.
