I have largely been interested in the field of computer architecture. I recently hit a realization - there is no meaning to computers if they don't make our life faster. This field of hardware optimizations, accelerators has rapidly evolved with the singular motivation of making computation faster cheaper and lesser without losing out on output accuracy and precision. Given the heavy demand for compute, optimization efforts have percolated every layer of the vertical compute chain. Recently I read a paper that reveals these multi-fold efforts for optimization made across different layers in the processing of deep neural networks.

In this blog I aim to provide a synapse of what I read and understood. I will serve to provide a keyhole view to anyone lookin to tap the door to this rapidly progressing field.
