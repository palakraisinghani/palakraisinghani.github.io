⏺ Here's the design direction I'm going with, before I touch anything:

  Theme: "Field Notes" — a scholar-engineer's spec document. The site currently
  reads as a soft literary personal page (Lora serif body, cursive script logo).
  I want to layer a technical, systems-design register on top without losing
  the warmth — the idea being your page reads like a cross between a design spec
  and a field journal:

  - Typography pairing: keep Lora/Fraunces for prose (that's your
  literary/philosophical voice), add JetBrains Mono for structural chrome —
  section labels, nav, dates, tags. Mono = "this is metadata," serif = "this is
  thought."
  - Numbered sections like a spec doc: §01 Brief Bio, §02 Interests, §03 Work...
  — a nod to the SPEC.md discipline you just introduced for the codebase
  itself, echoed in the content.
  - Blueprint texture: a very faint dot-grid background behind the hero, evoking
  architecture/circuit diagrams — subtle, not gimmicky.
  - Corner-bracket photo frame: your portrait gets architectural corner ticks
  (like a bounding box in a diagram) instead of a plain rounded shadow box.
  - Circuit-trace dividers: the plain <hr>s become dashed traces with a small
  centered node marker, replacing the generic gray line.
  - Tag chips: interest keywords (systems, cryptography, automata, HPC...)
  become small bordered mono pill-tags rather than plain prose.
  - Accent color: keep your existing navy (#003366) as primary, add a muted
  terracotta accent for warmth (art/culture/history side) used sparingly on
  nodes and hover states.

  Everything will be scoped to a .design-theme class and a new css/design.css
  file, and I'll copy index.html → index-design.html so the live site is
  untouched. Building it now.
